Questions

What partners ask first

Why 100% of month one and not a recurring share?

Because a full payment after your first referral is what makes creators keep going, and because for a new product the first paying month is the number that matters. Offers that are not monthly subscriptions use a flat bounty instead. If you would rather have a recurring split on an offer, tell us in the application. We are testing both in the pilot.

Why wait for the second renewal?

It covers the refund window without a clawback, it filters out sign-ups that were never real, and it means the studio only pays for subscribers who stayed. The hold is about 35 days, in line with the 30 to 90 day holds on most programs.

What happens if the studio retires a product?

Every listing is pre-funded before you see it. If an app is retired inside 12 months of listing, bounties you already earned are paid from that escrow on the normal schedule. You also get 60 days' notice before any change to the terms.

Does the discount come out of my bounty?

No. The 10% your audience gets is funded by the studio. Your bounty is 100% of what they actually paid for month one, and the $25 floor applies to that, so on most offers the floor is what you receive.

Can I subscribe through my own link?

No. Self-referrals are cancelled, and because bounties pay after the second renewal there is nothing to gain. Coupon sites, paid ads on a product's name and incentivised sign-ups are out for the same reason.

Do I need to be in the US?

No. We collect a W-9 or W-8BEN once at sign-up and pay by Wise or Stripe in your currency. EU partners invoice under reverse charge, which the monthly statement handles for you.

Who is Gildium?

A venture studio that builds and runs its own products: subscription apps, iOS apps and media properties such as Demilked. Every offer in the feed has real customers before it is listed, and the person who runs the product reads your feedback.
