Do marketplaces bring affiliates? Evidence is thin and vendor-sourced. Reditus: one founder "pleasantly surprised by the results" testing the marketplace in parallel. PartnerStack is the only one with a large, documented supply side, but is priced for $1M+ ARR. Rewardful (a competitor without a marketplace) argues marketplaces are low quality. No Reddit threads surfaced in search. [H] Small-vendor marketplaces (Reditus, Affonso, LinkJolt, LS Hub) are listing directories, not active matching; affiliate supply concentrates on high-ticket and recurring B2B programs.

## 2. Products explicitly for indie or vibecoded apps

- Creem published "How to Launch an Affiliate Program for Your Lovable App (2026 Guide)": a vendor-tool guide, no marketplace.
- ChatAds targets vibecoded apps but is the inverse: helps the app earn affiliate commissions by inserting links into AI chat responses.
- Lovable's own program pays "up to $100 for each first-time subscriber", a bounty structure targeting the same creators this marketplace would recruit.
- TinyAffiliate, Rapid Affiliates: lightweight tools "for bootstrapped SaaS startups and indie developers"; software only.
- Searches for "CPA influencer marketplace SaaS" and "influencers promote my app performance based" surfaced no dedicated product. [H] No incumbent combines (a) MoR and no-code billing integrations, (b) a curated creator supply, and (c) managed payouts for sub-$10k-MRR apps. The gap exists, but creators willing to promote unknown apps for CPA is the unproven side.

## 4. Distribution-for-a-cut analogues

- AppSumo: "vendors earn 95% of revenue from new buyers they bring ... 70% ... from returning AppSumo customers"; commonly cited 60/40; "evaluates about 10% of products"; "2,000-plus products" (2021); "revenue decline of 50% over the past two years" (disclosed Feb 2026).
- Product Hunt: "10 to 150 signups on launch day" for featured; median 7-day signups ~115; paid conversion "at or below 4 percent."
- Gumroad Discover: "10% + 50¢ direct, 30% via Discover", a referral-sized cut on marketplace-attributed sales.
- App stores: Apple 30% (15% Small Business up to $1M, 15% after year-one subs); Google Play 15% on all subscriptions since 2022.
- Amazon bounties: fixed "$3 to $30" per service signup; Audible "$10."
- Apple App Store affiliate program: removed apps and in-app content Oct 1, 2018.
- 100%-first-month precedents: HubSpot ("15% of the first year, or 100% of first month"), WP Engine ("$200 minimum or equal to the first month's payment"), Upmetrics (100% month one, 50% after), Omnisend (100% first payment, 30% after), Lovable (up to $100/subscriber). Systeme.io 60% lifetime; Kit 50% for 12 months. Industry median ~20 to 30% recurring. [H] 100%-first-month is an established, if rare, structure: credible to creators, and cheap for vendors with more than 2 months of retention.

## 5. Take rates and unit economics

Observed fees on payouts: Impact 2.5 to 3%; Rewardful 3%; Tolt 2%; Reditus 5%; PartnerStack 3 to 15%; Awin 2.5 to 3.5% (Access) to "20–30% override"; ShareASale 20%; Lemon Squeezy 2% of order; Kit 20% + 3.5%; Paved 30%; Gumroad Discover 30%; Collabstr 10%; AppSumo 30 to 60%. Guide benchmark: "network fees ... 20% to 30% of the commission value" when the network supplies affiliates; 2 to 5% when it is pure tooling.

[H] Defensible take: on a $20 first-month bounty, a 20 to 30% take is $4 to 6 gross per conversion, before Stripe Connect or PayPal payout costs (~$0.25 to 2 per payout; Dub charges "$2 withdrawal fee" for small withdrawals). Sub-10% takes (tooling tier) cannot fund fraud review and creator recruitment; 30%+ (AppSumo and Gumroad tier) is precedented only where the platform is the demand. Batching payouts monthly with a $50 threshold is the standard mitigation.
