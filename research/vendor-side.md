## 2. Commission norms

- Benchmarks: "median B2B SaaS affiliate commission is 20% and B2C SaaS at 15%"; "most SaaS programs fall in the 5-30% range" (https://track360.io/blog/saas-affiliate-commission-rates-benchmark-2026). Rewardful: recurring "typically sit between 20% and 30%"; "Most SaaS programs use a 6- or 12-month fixed recurring term" (https://www.rewardful.com/articles/affiliate-commission-explained).
- Bounty norm: "usually 50-100% of the first month's revenue… if your plan costs $49/month, you might offer a $100 bounty" (https://www.linkjolt.io/blog/saas-affiliate-program).
- Programs paying 100% of first payment: HubSpot ("15% of the first year, or 100% of first month"), WP Engine ("100% commission… on the first month's payment", up to 200%/$500 cap), Omnisend ("100% of the first payment for monthly plans with 30% recurring"), ProProfs ("100% of the first month's subscription"), Upmetrics ("100% in the first month and 50% in the following months"), Coursera ("up to 100% of the first two payments"). Sources: https://tapfiliate.com/blog/saas-affiliate-marketing/, https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/saas-affiliate-commission-rates/, https://smartreach.io/blog/best-saas-affiliate-programs/. Endorsely natively supports "100% of the first month (or 50% of the first 3 months)" (https://www.endorsely.com/). Tapfiliate's rationale: "Affiliates who see an immediate, full commission check after their first referral activate at significantly higher rates" (https://tapfiliate.com/blog/a-complete-guide-for-saas-affiliate-commissions/).
- Vibecoding platforms' own programs: Lovable pays "20% Recurring For 12m" (https://taprefer.com/lovable-affiliate-program/lovable/); Base44 pays "$100 commission on referrals that convert to paid users within a month" (https://base44.com/affiliates).
- Holds and clawback: Rewardful "default due period… is 30 days following the sale… match this period to your company's refund policy"; full refund deletes the commission (https://www.rewardful.com/automated-refund-handling). Lemon Squeezy: "commissions are held for 30 days" (https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/affiliates/payouts). Card disputes: "within 120 days", some reason codes "up to 540 days" (https://www.chargeflow.io/chargebacks-101/stripe-chargebacks); practitioner advice: "most programs hold 30 to 60 days… then accept that a rare late dispute becomes a clawback" (https://dev.to/mihirkanzariya/dont-pay-affiliate-commissions-the-instant-a-payment-succeeds-hold-them-through-the-refund-window-a4a).
- Cookies: Rewardful default "60 days following the initial click", customizable (https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/2155812-how-long-are-referral-cookies-valid); 30 days is the other common default.
- Fraud: self-referral is an affiliate who "uses their own affiliate link to purchase your product—essentially getting a discount"; Rewardful "automatically identifies and deactivates suspected self-referrals" (https://www.rewardful.com/self-referral-fraud-detection). Coupon leakage: "the coupon-site affiliate scrapes the last-click attribution at checkout, and you pay commission plus discount on customers you never needed to acquire" (https://track360.io/blog/saas-affiliate-fraud-detection-2026). Honey "lost more than seven million Chrome users and around 7,000 merchant partners" after the last-click-hijack exposé (https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/honey-loses-over-7-million-users-and-7000-stores-following-youtubers-viral-expose-3397619/).

## 3. Unit economics that decide publisher attractiveness

- Price points: successful vibecoded and indie apps "run monthly or annual subscriptions priced $9 to $99 per month" (https://superframeworks.com/articles/vibe-coding-tipping-point-what-founders-need-to-know); Creem's Lovable guide example uses "$29/month".
- Free-to-paid: ChartMogul: "3%-5% is a GOOD conversion rate for a freemium self-serve product, and 8%-12% is GREAT" (https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/). Poyar/ChartMogul/ProductLed 2026 survey: "one-in-four freemium products convert less than 2.5%… median sits at 8%" (https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/free-to-paid-conversion-report). Lenny: "self-serve freemium models convert at 6-8%" (https://userpilot.com/blog/saas-average-conversion-rate/).
- Churn: ChartMogul: for ARPA "<$25, the median monthly customer churn rate is 6.1%", falling to 2.2% above $500 (https://chartmogul.com/blog/good-customer-churn-rate/). Baremetrics, 600 SMB SaaS: "average monthly churn of 7.5%" (https://www.hubifi.com/blog/calculate-saas-churn-rate). Indie benchmark: "median indie SaaS churns 6-8% monthly" (https://savemrr.co/blog/good-churn-rate-indie-saas). Consumer apps: "Nearly 30% of annual app subscriptions are canceled in the first month" (https://www.emarketer.com/content/apps-need-solve-customer-retention-issues-amid-high-churn-short-term-subscriptions).
- [H] Worked implication: at $15/mo and 6.5% monthly churn, gross LTV is about $15/0.065, roughly $230; a 100%-first-month bounty ($15) is about 6.5% of LTV, while 20% for 12 months on the same cohort yields about $15 x 0.2 x ~8.6 surviving months, roughly $26. So the "100% first month" offer is cheap for the vendor and only marginally worse for the publisher than a 12-month rev share. But a $15 bounty per conversion, at 2 to 5% landing-page-to-paid, means a publisher earns roughly $0.30 to $0.75 per click-through, which is the real attractiveness constraint.

## 4. Integration reality

- Stripe: Rewardful passes the referral UUID "as the client_reference_id parameter when redirecting to checkout"; must set "customer_creation as 'always', otherwise we won't be able to associate a referral" (https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/9014067-integration-using-stripe-server-side-checkout); Payment Links also supported (https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/6684154-integration-with-stripe-payment-links). Critique: "Rewardful relies on client-side tracking with no server-side fallback, and cookies can be blocked by Safari ITP" (https://dev.to/mihirkanzariya/tracking-affiliate-referrals-on-stripe-without-third-party-cookies-5fh9), matching the HN complaint above.
- AI builders: Rewardful has a dedicated article: the tracking script "should be copied and given to the AI"; "Loveable AI and Base44 AI usually generate Stripe checkout forms by using the Stripe Checkout Session method"; for React/Next the AI "should refactor/modify the tracking script" (https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/12682850-integration-with-ai-generated-websites). Lovable supports "built-in Paddle or Stripe integration" plus its own Lovable Payments (https://docs.lovable.dev/features/payments, https://freemius.com/blog/what-is-lovable-payments/); Base44 offers a Stripe connection with sandbox testing (https://base44.com/blog/base44-stripe-integration). No Lovable or Base44 first-party docs on affiliate tracking were found; a "paste this snippet plus add client_reference_id" prompt is feasible but unverified across builders [H].
- MoR platforms: Lemon Squeezy has native affiliates; "3% fee for each order referred by an affiliate" on top of platform fee (https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/affiliates-for-merchants/fees). Creem: built-in via Affonso, "additional 2% fee on the transaction amount"; "Trials… earn nothing until the first real charge lands" (https://dodopayments.com/blogs/creem-io-review, https://affonso.io/creem-affiliate-program). Polar: "no built-in affiliate management system"; Affonso integration, or "?reference_id=CODE" on checkout links copied "into order metadata" (https://rekomi.com/blog/polar-affiliate-program, https://dodopayments.com/blogs/polar-sh-review). Dodo: affiliates via Affonso and Dub Partners (https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/affiliates). Paddle "discontinued their in-house affiliate functionality"; Rewardful supports Paddle Classic only, Tolt and FirstPromoter support Paddle Billing (https://www.rewardful.com/paddle, https://tolt.com/paddle-affiliate-program).
- Takeaway [H]: a marketplace can plausibly be tracking-agnostic by reading Stripe webhooks (client_reference_id or customer metadata) and MoR webhooks (reference_id or Affonso), rather than shipping its own cookie script.

## 5. Legal, tax, money movement

- FTC: 2023 Endorsement Guides require "clear and conspicuous" disclosure that is "unavoidable"; affiliate commissions are a "material connection"; "Brands can be held liable for their endorsers' failure to disclose"; civil penalty "currently $53,088 per violation" (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking, https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/ftc-influencer-guidelines).
- US tax: 1099-NEC threshold "is $2,000… raised from $600 by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act for payments made on or after January 1, 2026" (https://www.gigapay.com/blog/1099-nec-in-2026). Foreign payees: "no 1099 is required, and the payment is normally not subject to chapter 3 withholding" when W-8BEN is on file and services are performed outside the US (https://www.taxesforexpats.com/articles/foreign-business/form-1099-for-foreign-contractors.html).
- EU VAT: cross-border B2B commissions use reverse charge: "Both of you have a VAT number… the reverse-charge mechanism is used" (https://jeangalea.com/affiliate-income-eu-moss-vat-treatmentrules/); networks issue self-billing invoices stating "Reverse Charge" (https://avelonetwork.com/knowledge-base/brand/how-vat-works-on-affiliate-commissions). Non-VAT-registered creators are a compliance edge case [H].
- Money transmission: collecting from vendors and paying publishers risks MSB status unless structured under the "agent of the payee" exemption, which "does not exist in all states" (https://www.moderntreasury.com/learn/what-is-an-agent-of-the-payee-exemption, https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2018/06/money-transmission-in-the-payment-facilitator-mode). Stripe Connect lets platforms "leverage its money transmission licenses" and provides 1099 generation (https://stripe.com/connect/marketplaces).
- How incumbents do it: PartnerStack "consolidates all approved commissions into a single monthly invoice… you have 7 days to review it… automatically charged… and dispersed to your partners" via PayPal, Stripe or direct deposit (https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009313233-Paying-your-partner-commissions). Impact requires brands to pre-fund; if underfunded "the payment will not be released and will be marked as Overdue" (https://help.impact.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001237476-partner-payments-explained-from-action-to-payout). Tolt: manual CSV to "PayPal Mass Payouts or Wise Batch Payment", or auto-payouts where "Tolt sends you an invoice… Then, Tolt pays all your affiliates" (https://help.tolt.com/en/articles/9921292-how-to-select-payout-methods). Rewardful: mass-payout CSV or "Managed Payouts" (https://www.rewardful.com/managed-payouts). Trolley and Tipalti collect W-9/W-8 and generate "1099-K, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, & 1042-S" (https://trolley.com/tax/irs-compliance/).

## Bottom line

Facts support: (a) affiliate software is widely viewed as necessary but insufficient, recruitment is the unsolved job; (b) 100%-first-month bounties are an established, respected structure; (c) tracking is solvable via checkout reference IDs across Stripe and every MoR; (d) payouts and tax are commoditized (Stripe Connect, Trolley). Unverified: that vibecoders specifically feel this pain, and that $5 to 30/mo apps generate enough per-click earnings to attract publishers [H].
