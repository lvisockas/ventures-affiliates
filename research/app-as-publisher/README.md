# Superseded direction: the app as affiliate publisher

These three reports researched the reverse framing, where the vibecoded app earns commissions by linking out to other merchants. The operator clarified on 2026-09-02 that the intended use case is the opposite: the vibecoded subscription app is the merchant, and the platform supplies publishers and influencers who promote it for a bounty such as 100% of the first month.

Kept because the demand-side data on builder population, monetization intent and the distribution bottleneck still applies, and because the network policy and take-rate material is useful context.
