# Publisher and influencer supply for SaaS and AI-app affiliate offers

Fetched 2026-09-02 from search snippets (page fetches blocked). "H:" marks hypotheses. Re-verify before citing.

## 1. Who promotes small SaaS and AI apps today

AI tool directories: primarily pay-to-list, not commission-driven.

| Directory | Listing price | Traffic | Affiliate behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| There's An AI For That | "$347, and it is a one-off payment" (theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured); plus "$300 PPC bonus for tools that launch exclusively on their platform first" | Similarweb 4.9M visits (Jul 2025); Semrush "10.05M visits" | Sells ads via BuySellAds and Passionfroot; newsletter "1.2M+ subscribers". No public evidence it takes vendor affiliate links. |
| Futurepedia | "one-time fee of $497" verified listing; "$297 per month for featured placement" | Declining: "326.62K monthly visits with a ~30% decrease" (Apr 2025), 394.81K (Oct 2025) | Disclosure: "participates in various affiliate advertising programs and may receive compensation"; runs "Skill Leap" YouTube advertiser intake. |
| Toolify | "$99 to list one AI tool (no free tier)" | "5.1 million monthly visits" (self-reported) | Paid placement model. |
| TopAI.tools / aitools.fyi | n/a | n/a | Run their own affiliate programs ("25% of recurring revenues for life for every paid submission", topaitools.com/affiliates); they monetize vendors, not commissions. |
| Long tail (Aitoolnet $9.9, MLWeekly $49, aitoolsdirectory.com) | $10 to $100 | small | aitoolsdirectory.com: "uses affiliate links, meaning if you click and purchase through them, they may receive a commission" |

Takeaway: top directories are ad and listing businesses; only long-tail directories run on affiliate links. H: directories would use a marketplace as a supplementary revenue layer, not a primary one.

Newsletters: sponsorship-priced, not CPA.
- Ben's Bites: "main ad spot… $2,000… tools section costs $1,200… 'unclassified' ad option is available for $200" (thenewsletternewsletter.substack.com, Dec 2024).
- TLDR: "primary placement ranges from $6K to $24K per sent, equating to $7-$26 CPM" (unanswered.io).
- The Rundown: "Full Email Takeovers can expect 900k-1M email impressions and 25,000-50,000 clicks"; pricing undisclosed.
- Superhuman: "ad spots sell out consistently at six figures a month" (passionfroot.me blog).
- CPA in newsletters exists but is priced high: "$10-$100 per acquisition is common for newsletter sponsorships" (beehiiv.com/blog). beehiiv Ad Network is "CPM or CPC pricing"; beehiiv Boosts pay "$1.50-$5.00 per acquired email".
- Small lists: "Small B2B and niche lists command $20 to $60 per 1,000 opens" (lilachbullock.com). Affiliate income is one of "two to four revenue streams" for successful publishers.

YouTube "AI tools" creators: heavy affiliate users, via recurring programs.
- "Most AI tool affiliate programs in 2026 offer 20-40% recurring commissions… creators… can earn $2,000-10,000/month from recurring commissions alone" (outlierkit.com).
- Futurepedia's YouTube AdSense is estimated at only "$4.95K monthly" (vidiq.com). H: affiliate and sponsor revenue dominates for these channels.

TikTok creators: structurally blocked from SaaS via TikTok Shop: "TikTok Shop's Prohibited Products Policy states that virtual or digital products are prohibited unless a seller is approved under the Virtual Goods category… External affiliate programs… run through a link destination in your bio" (favly.com). Favly is an emerging link-in-bio storefront "built for AI, SaaS and creator-tool creators."

Review and comparison sites: G2 and Capterra sell leads and clicks to vendors, not affiliate: "vendors can pay per click ($2/click minimum, $500/month minimum budget…)" (blastra.io). Independent blogs run the 20 to 30% recurring programs listed in roundups.

Discord and Reddit: Discord monetizes via sponsorship ("10,000-member gaming server… $200 to $500 per announcement"; "affiliate income ranges from $200–$2,000/month for servers with 5,000–20,000 active members", discords.ai). Reddit mods are unpaid and monetization is contested; no evidence of a structured mod-affiliate channel.

## 2. What publishers require, and their complaints

Requirements:
- Cookie window: "30 to 90 day cookie window is common for SaaS… Cookie durations under 14 days are considered red flags" (affililist.com).
- "EPC is the most important metric a network provides" (same source). "Affiliates research the program's payment track record before investing content in promotion."
- Audience fit trumps rate: "A 20% commission on a product an audience actually needs outperforms a 60% commission on a product they don't."
- Tracking and payout trust: "even the most generous offers will fall flat" without "rock-solid tracking" (postaffiliatepro.com).

Complaints and program churn:
- Jasper: "program ending on January 26, 2025… final payout… April 15, 2025"; earlier "abandoned its guarantee of lifetime commissions" (Nov 2023).
- Founder-side churn: "SaaS founders… run it for four months, and quietly shut the program down" (rewardful.com). "Most premature SaaS affiliate programs don't fail obviously—they just fade away".
- Payouts: PartnerStack "2.0 out of 5 on Trustpilot, where payout delays and account bans dominate the complaints" (alidropship.com); one affiliate reported PartnerStack "not processing a single commission while the other two platforms generated over $1000" (b2bsaasreviews.com). Commissions "often held for 30 to 90 days to account for refund windows."
- Low-ticket sentiment: "Most low-ticket programs are time-consuming and deliver small returns per sale" (redtrack.io).
- Apple cut App Store affiliate "from 7 percent to 2.5 percent": a canonical "vendor changed terms" scar.
- "100% first month" is a recognized format: HubSpot offers "15% of the first year, or 100% of first month"; Shopify "100% commission on the first two monthly payments"; bounties "usually range from 50-100% of the first month's revenue" (tapfiliate.com). These are large brands. H: the format is accepted, the issuer is the trust problem.

Verbatim r/Affiliatemarketing, affLIFT or STM threads about unknown SaaS programs could not be surfaced. Treat forum sentiment as not directly evidenced.

## 3. How publishers discover offers today

- PartnerStack Marketplace: "more than 300 partner programs and 80,000+ active partners"; vendor pricing starts high. No independent data on whether small vendors get partners; PartnerStack's own claim: "vendors saw a 541% increase in partner-sourced revenue after one year" (self-reported, survivorship-biased). Partner reviewers ask to "add more affiliate programs."
- impact.com: "300K+ vetted and verified global partners"; brands pay "Essential plan starts at $500/month" plus "2.5% fee on every commission". Criticism: "doesn't vet their partners… finding the right sector-specific partners can take considerable time" (surgeaffiliate.com).
- ShareASale to Awin: "by end of 2025, the ShareASale platform will close entirely."
- Reditus: B2B-only marketplace; "6,000+ SaaS-focused affiliates" / "26,000+ B2B SaaS affiliates" (two different self-reported figures).
- Lemon Squeezy Affiliate Hub: merchant flips a switch and "their program is in front of thousands of affiliates". Closest existing analogue for vibecoded apps, but limited to LS-billed products.
- Directories: Affiliate Program DB, Lasso, AffiliateOtter, saasaffiliate.com, zPlatform ("70 ranked" AI programs), Apify "AI Tool Affiliate Tracker". One source: "There is no canonical directory of affiliate programs in the AI dev tool space" (layer3labs.io).
- Indie reality: "so far only one affiliate has signed up" (indiehackers.com, Notion Invoice); "the majority of affiliates who sign up for any program never generate a single referral" (getreditus.com); "Top 10% of affiliates produce 70% of revenue" (rewardful.com).

## 4. Publisher-side economics

- SaaS EPC: "$1.20–$3.50 EPC" (dollarpocket.com); "average SaaS referral-to-sale conversion rate of 0.8%" (Rewardful).
- Implied: a $15 bounty at 0.8% click-to-sale is $0.12 EPC, below the "$0.50 indicates a struggling program" threshold. Even at 3% conversion it is $0.45. H: 100%-first-month on a $15 app is uncompetitive on EPC unless conversion is exceptional.
- Comparables: Audible "$5 for eligible free trial… $10 for eligible paid"; Prime Video "$3 bounty for a 30-day free trial"; NordVPN "100% for 1-month, 40% for 1-year and 2-year… renewals at 30%"; Notion "50% of all payments for the first 12 months… 180 days" cookie; Jasper was 25 to 30% recurring; Merlin, Writesonic, Manychat 30% recurring; Semrush "$200 per trial", Shopify "$150 per merchant".
- Average Rewardful program commission: "22.1% for programs under $100K… 24.5% for top-tier."
- Creator and CPA alternatives: UGC "median rate for a single UGC video in 2025 is about $175" (billo.app); micro-influencers "$200–$800 per TikTok video"; app CPI "North America is US$5.28", influencer channel "$4.2" CPI; "Average CPA ranges from $5 to $20". Kale pays "$25-40 per video" but one creator "made exactly $256 after… over a year" (medium.com/@casivagher).
- TikTok Shop: "effective average around 13% in the US"; "1% to 80%… set by sellers"; "minimum of 1,000 followers."

H: A $10 to 30 bounty competes with Audible-tier bounties on absolute value but loses to 30%-recurring SaaS programs on lifetime value; creators who post for $25 to 40 fixed (Kale) exist, but those are guaranteed payments, not CPA.

## Bottom line

Facts: the highest-value AI and SaaS publishers (TAAFT, Rundown, Superhuman, TLDR) sell fixed-price inventory; commission-driven supply is concentrated in YouTube and blog creators on 20 to 40% recurring programs; TikTok's native affiliate excludes SaaS; existing SaaS marketplaces (PartnerStack, Impact) gate small vendors behind $500+/month fees while Lemon Squeezy and Reditus are the low-friction analogues; affiliates' documented pain is vendor churn and payout reliability.

H1: The marketplace's defensible value to publishers is platform-guaranteed payout plus aggregated fresh offers, not the bounty size. H2: A $15 one-time bounty will underperform on EPC; offers may need hybrid (bounty plus recurring) or vendor-escrowed funds to earn creator attention. H3: Best initial supply is small YouTube, Substack and Favly-style creators and long-tail directories, not the marquee newsletters.
